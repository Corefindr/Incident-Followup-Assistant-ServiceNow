# Demo Guide

Use generated test records only.

## Demo Scenario

1. Create a fake incident with a test caller.
2. Move the incident to On Hold with hold reason Awaiting Caller.
3. Confirm a follow-up record is created.
4. Open the Agent Command Center.
5. Record an attempt with outcome `Email sent`.
6. Confirm a structured work note appears on the incident.
7. Adjust the due date to simulate Due and Overdue states.
8. Open the Team Overview dashboard.
9. Show counts by Upcoming, Due, Overdue, owner, and repeat cycle.
10. Resolve the follow-up when caller response is received.

## Talk Track

- The tool turns an informal reminder process into a visible operational queue.
- Agents can update follow-ups without hunting through multiple incident tabs.
- Managers get early visibility before ageing pending incidents become escalations.
- The app tracks repeat pending cycles so recurring caller-delay patterns become measurable.
